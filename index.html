Overview

Complete documentation for the Deposit feature — the money-in flow that lets a customer add funds to their account. It covers first-time deposit (FTD) at onboarding and regular deposits, amount entry with quick-amount chips and min/max limits, payment method selection and management, card entry and scanning, 3DS/SCA verification, error and edge-case states, bonus/offer signposting, deposit success, and Responsible Gambling deposit limits (Spending Limits / Deposit Cap).

Deposit lives in Player Experience › Payments and is surfaced from several entry points (onboarding, Home/Menu, Quick Deposit in the betslip or in-game menu, and the Rewards drawer). The header adapts per entry point (progress bar during onboarding, brand header in-app) but the amount-entry module stays consistent.

Principles

Derived from the source content's recurring intent; confirm with the feature lead before approval.

  • Keep amount entry consistent across every entry point, even when the surrounding header changes.
  • Make the valid deposit range unmissable, and never let an invalid amount progress.
  • Treat Responsible Gambling interventions as first-class, distinct from payment errors, and escalate proportionately from soft (dismissible) to hard (blocking).
  • Preserve the customer's entered amount and selected method across interruptions (e.g. failed SCA) so they can retry immediately.

Guidelines

Feature intent

The customer wants to add funds to their account quickly and with confidence — whether it's their first-ever deposit during onboarding or a routine top-up from the betslip. The flow must make the valid amount range and selected method obvious, support adding/verifying payment methods, and clear regulatory and safer-gambling checks without losing the customer's progress.

User mental model

  • Customers think in terms of "how much do I want to add and with which method" — the amount and the payment method are the two things they expect to control.
  • They expect quick-amount chips to be shortcuts, not commitments, and expect the primary CTA to stay inert until the amount is valid.
  • Returning customers expect their default/stored method to be pre-selected.
  • Customers distinguish "the payment failed" from "I'm being asked to slow down for safer-gambling reasons".
  • When launched from the betslip or in-game, customers expect a compact Quick Deposit rather than the full flow.

Hard constraints

NEVER allow a deposit that breaches a customer's monthly loss limit or deposit cap. (Layer 1)
NEVER process a deposit that requires SCA without completing the external 3DS/SCA check. (Layer 1)
NEVER apply a customer-requested limit increase instantly — increases are subject to review. (Layer 1)
NEVER enable the Deposit CTA when the entered amount is outside the method's min/max range. (Layer 3)
NEVER allow a new payment method to be added without processing a deposit, except via My Payment Methods. (Layer 3)

Examples

Every positive example is paired with a counter-example that cites the rule or principle it violates (Req 8.6).

Amount below method minimum
Correct
For a £15-minimum method, entering £5 keeps the amount in Min error and the Deposit CTA disabled with "Min deposit £15".
Counter-example
Showing a £5 quick-amount chip that populates the field and enables Deposit below the method minimum — violates B-001.
Monthly loss limit reached
Correct
Deposits are paused and only an "Increase limit" request (subject to review) is offered.
Counter-example
Applying the requested limit increase instantly and letting the deposit through — violates R-001 and the Layer 1 hard constraint.
SCA interruption
Correct
After a blocked pop-up, the customer sees "Open window", and their amount and method are retained for immediate retry.
Counter-example
Discarding the entered amount and method after a failed security check — violates B-007.
Bonus-ineligible method
Correct
Selecting Skrill while an offer is active shows a "Bonus Ineligible" badge before the customer commits.
Counter-example
Letting the customer deposit with a bonus-ineligible method and silently voiding the offer — violates B-005.
