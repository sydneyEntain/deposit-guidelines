When to Show Regular Deposit vs Quick Deposit vs My Payment Methods

ScenarioWhere it happens
Adding funds with a saved payment method ✅ in regular deposit if the payment method isn't QD eligible, or if the customer navigated to deposit from the top or side nav.

✅ in quick deposit if the customer is adding funds within the betslip or from the in-game menu.
First deposit during onboarding (FTD) ✅ in regular deposit (with bonus signposting).
First deposit if launched from in-game or betslip ✅ in regular deposit.
Adding or verifying a new payment method only ✅ in regular deposit (cannot add a payment method without processing a deposit).

✅ in quick deposit if they launch from the correct place and are attempting to add a QD-approved method such as Apple Pay.
Editing or deleting a payment method ✅ in my payment methods.

Amount Context (Deposit Amount component)

The amount display is a shared component with four states:

StateBehaviour
Valid or ZeroDefault entry; large £ value, min/max shown below
Min errorAmount below the method minimum (e.g. "Min deposit £5")
Max errorAmount above the allowed maximum for the method/limit

Each amount-entry screen has variants with and without the custom keypad and quick-amount chips visible.

Amount Entry

The core screen. Heading "Deposit", a large centred £ amount, the active limits shown as e.g. "£20 min | £200 max" (per-method minimums such as "Min deposit £5" also appear), the selected payment method row, a primary "Deposit" CTA, and a custom numeric keypad.

With Quick-Amount Chips

Quick-amount chips set common values in one tap. Two chip sets appear across layouts:

  • £20 / £50 / £100
  • £5 / £10 / £20

Chips sit above the keypad and populate the amount field.

Empty vs Entered Value

  • Empty: amount shows the zero/placeholder state; "Deposit" is disabled.
  • Enter value: amount updates live via the keypad; "Deposit" enables once the amount is at or above the method minimum.

Add Payment Method

  • Short list: most relevant methods (e.g. Apple Pay "Popular", Debit/credit cards, Pay by bank) with a "More Payment Methods" button.
  • Full list: the complete method list on its own screen.
  • Bonus eligibility: methods that disqualify an active offer show a "Bonus Ineligible" badge before selection.

Change Method

Opened as a bottom sheet from the amount screen. Contains:

  • My payment methods — the customer's stored methods.
  • Add payment method — methods available to add, each with its min deposit.
  • Inactive methods — methods that can't currently be used, grouped under an informational alert.

3DS / SCA & Verification

Deposits requiring Strong Customer Authentication route through an external security check (a pop-up browser window).

Check not completed "Deposit not completed — You didn't complete the external security check, so this deposit didn't process. Please try again and complete the check, or use a different method."
Pop-up blocked "Window blocked from opening — We have detected that your pop-up blocker has stopped the new browser window from opening." with an "Open window" action.

In both cases the customer stays on the deposit screen with their amount and method retained so they can retry immediately.

Bonus / Offer Signposting

  • Header signpost: e.g. "£20 in Free Bets — When you bet £5 on football", with a progress bar tracking onboarding/deposit steps and an "18+ T&Cs apply" reference.
  • Method-level eligibility: methods that void the offer are badged "Bonus Ineligible".

Deposit Success

  • Confirmation: "Deposit success!" with "£{amount} has been added to your account" (e.g. "£35 has been added to your account").
  • New balance pill: e.g. "New Balance £35.00".
  • Offer reminder where applicable: e.g. "Bet £5+ on sports to get £20 in Free Bets. Take me to offer details."
  • Full T&Cs modal — "General Terms and Conditions" bottom sheet: How the offer works, Promotional Terms and Conditions, Promotional Period, Who qualifies, How to claim, Restrictions on your free bet, Important Terms.

Responsible Gambling — Deposit Limits (Spending Limits)

Deposit is gated by Responsible Gambling controls. The "Spending Limits" screen (Deposit Cap) shows current limits and their status.

Deposits paused "Monthly loss limit reached. Resets 1 July 2026."
  • Monthly loss limit: e.g. "500 / 500 GBP", "Limit reached", with reset date and "Manage limit >".
  • Annual deposit cap: e.g. "125 / 7,500 GBP", "2% used", with reset date and "Manage limit >".
  • Cross-brand note: "These limits protect your spending across all Entain accounts. You can request a review, but changes are not guaranteed."
  • What you can do: "Request a limit review" (secondary CTA) — "We will review your request within 24 hours" — and "Set additional limits".
  • Support link: "Responsible gambling support".
When a limit is reached during a deposit attempt, the customer sees an alert such as "You've reached your monthly loss limit — Your monthly loss limit of £500 has been reached. This limit will be reset on the first of next month, or you can request an increase to your limit." with an "Increase limit" action. Any increase is subject to review, not applied instantly.
